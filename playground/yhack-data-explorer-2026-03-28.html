Legal Basis for Provenance Tags

LAW Hospital Price Transparency (45 CFR 180)

Effective Jan 2021, strengthened Jan 2026. Requires hospitals to publish 5 standard charge types in machine-readable format. Penalty: $300/day non-compliance. 91% post something; only 21% fully compliant.

LAW Transparency in Coverage (45 CFR 147.211)

Effective Jul 2022. Requires insurers to publish in-network negotiated rates in JSON (MRF). Monthly updates. 100+ TB across all payers. BCBS MA accessibility: 100% per Payerset audit.
