⭐ Winning Rules — Keep These in Mind While Brainstorming
FROM JUDGES

Judges decide in 60 seconds. Lead with WHY, not WHAT. A personal story beats a technical explanation every time. (Devpost: 5 seasoned judges)

THE WOW TEST

If you can't describe your demo's "wow moment" in one sentence, your idea isn't differentiated enough. Shepherd won because "blindfolded obstacle course" = instant understanding.

SPONSOR = CORE

If you can remove the sponsor API and the product still works, judges will notice. Sponsor tech must be load-bearing, not decorative. (Minerva won 2 tracks this way)

SCOPE KILLS

Winners cut 60% of features by hour 6. A half-working feature is worse than a missing feature — it breaks during demo and destroys credibility. (Oleksandr, 47% win rate)

NOT ANOTHER CHATBOT

If the core UX is "text input → text output," you look like every other team. Add a physical, visual, or voice interaction that judges can FEEL. (HospiTwin applied digital twin concept from manufacturing)

TIME SPLIT: 40/30/30

40% building, 30% polish/demo, 30% pitch. Most teams do 95/5/0. Sze Yu Sim: "I spend 30% of hackathon time on the pitch." Plan the video BEFORE coding.

OUR EDGE

A real doctor + multilingual team (EN/ZH/FR/AR) + AI agent builder. Most teams are 4 CS majors building chatbots. Our domain credibility can't be faked.

PROVEN PATTERN, NEW DOMAIN

HospiTwin applied manufacturing digital twins to hospitals. We apply Artopath's knowledge graph to health records. Repurposing known patterns = "innovative" to judges.
